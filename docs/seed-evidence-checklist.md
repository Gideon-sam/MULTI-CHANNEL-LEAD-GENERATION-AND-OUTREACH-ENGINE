# Your owned-account seed test: actions and screenshots

This extends seed-test-runbook.md. The existing scheduler, tracker and reply ledger never send messages. Use only sender and recipient accounts you own on email, LinkedIn and Instagram. No account passwords or API keys belong in files.

1. Copy config/seed_accounts.template.json to config/seed_accounts.local.json. Enter your actual sender/recipient identifiers for all three channels and confirm ownership. Screenshot the account/profile names; omit passwords and unrelated messages.
2. Pick a future time with +05:30 offset. Run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m incubrix_engine seed plan --campaign my_owned_seed_1 --start YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS+05:30 --base-url http://127.0.0.1:8081`. Use a new campaign ID for a new test. Screenshot seed_private/plan.csv showing message IDs, channels and due times. Keep tracking tokens private.
3. Start `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m incubrix_engine seed serve --port 8081`. Open http://127.0.0.1:8081/health on the same machine. Screenshot its `ok` response and the terminal. Receiving accounts must open localhost links on that same machine; localhost on your phone points to your phone. A remote test needs a separately configured reachable endpoint.
4. Choose a draft from output/outreach/segment_channel_sequences.csv. Replace every placeholder. Send only to the owned receiving account; the creator-specific drafts are evidence artifacts and must not be sent to creators. Insert the tracking URL belonging to that channel's first message.
5. In each native service, schedule that message if supported. Screenshot recipient, message text, tracking URL and scheduled time. If your account cannot schedule DMs, screenshot the limitation and leave native scheduling proof incomplete. A local due task is not proof of native sending.
6. Record the real scheduling evidence: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m incubrix_engine seed record --message-id ACTUAL_MESSAGE_ID --event native_scheduled --evidence seed_private/CHANNEL-scheduled.png`. The evidence file must exist. Do not record unsupported scheduling as successful.
7. At the due time, check `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m incubrix_engine seed due`. After the native service actually sends, screenshot the sent message with recipient/time; record it with the same command and `--event sent --evidence seed_private/CHANNEL-sent.png`.
8. In the receiving account on the tracker machine, open the link after the send is recorded. Screenshot the received message and redirected URL with channel-specific UTM parameters. A click before `sent` is logged as a pre-send test and does not satisfy the tracking requirement.
9. Reply from the owned receiving account. Screenshot both the reply and its time. Record `--event reply --evidence seed_private/CHANNEL-reply.png`. Inspect `seed due` to verify later follow-ups for that recipient are suppressed.
10. Repeat steps 4–9 for all three channels. Run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m incubrix_engine seed report`. Save the actual output and screenshot it. Every channel needs native_scheduled, sent, click and reply for the same message. Do not substitute unit-test results or events from different messages.
11. Review the saved evidence, then fill the workbook's seed-test TODO with the private proof location and actual result. If any step is unsupported or fails, document the limitation and leave the claim incomplete.

Screenshot names per channel: ownership, scheduled (or scheduling-unavailable), sent, received, click, reply, suppression. Final screenshot: seed report. Retain these under ignored seed_private/; share a redacted evidence copy privately with assessors.
