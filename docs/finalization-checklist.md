# Candidate-only finalization checklist

Do not mark any item complete without a working evidence link.

1. Enter the candidate ID, registered email, college/degree, assessment start time and submission time in `00_START`.
2. Sign in through the product invitation. Re-run the five product observations against the authenticated product, capture evidence, and replace any public-page-only observation that does not match reality.
3. Run the three-channel seed test only on accounts you own. Capture timestamped scheduling, tracking-link and reply-log evidence; then change the seed self-check from `No` to `Yes`.
4. Reproduce the recorded normal, repeat, refreshed-new-input and recovery runs from `verification/` in your technical video. Rows 19–21 of `03_ENGINE_LAUNCH` cite the recorded evidence.
5. Create a private repository or access-controlled shared folder and paste its URL into the workbook.
6. Upload `output/`, `outreach/`, `launch-kit.md` and the completed workbook; paste the main folder and launch-kit URLs.
7. Record both videos from `docs/video-runbook.md` without editing and paste their URLs.
8. Review the AI/manual work disclosure and personally accept the declaration. No tool should accept it for you.
9. Open 20 randomly selected leads, not hand-picked ones, and confirm every evidence URL, country, creator fit, recency, commercial signal and public contact.
10. Recalculate the workbook in Excel/LibreOffice and confirm its values match `output/validation_report.json`.
