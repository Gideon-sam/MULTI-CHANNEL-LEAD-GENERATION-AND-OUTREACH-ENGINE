# What only the candidate can complete

- Use the authenticated IncuBrix product. Record five observations with actual evidence and three prioritized opportunities. Fill the blank cells in In cuBrix's review workbook; do not substitute public-site research for hands-on use.
- Open the fixed-seed audit sample named in output/submission_status.json. Verify every row, business-contact purpose/activity, country, creator identity, backup route and content evidence. Enter the verdicts and run the scorer. Resolve evidence_gaps.csv and link-check failures. A 40-row audit does not verify every other row.
- Use only sender/recipient accounts you own on email, LinkedIn and Instagram. Follow seed-evidence-checklist.md and capture scheduling, sending, clicks, replies and suppression. Leave unsupported scheduling or failed events incomplete; no software fixture proves native-platform behavior.
- Record two unedited videos: product walkthrough at most five minutes, technical demo at most eight minutes. Include normal run, refresh on new input and a clearly labelled simulated failure recovery.
- Complete your identity, declaration, self-score and private reviewer-access links. Confirm actual spend from billing records; do not enter an estimate as measured cost. Do not put keys or passwords in the workbook or repository.

The exceptional bar is 5,000 verified creators, 250 complete Priority A sequences, 90%+ qualification and 95%+ channel accuracy in the assessor's audit, a measured 5,000-row refresh and evidence of creator response by channel. Discovery counts and owned-account test replies cannot satisfy these requirements. Follow the brief's prohibition on contacting real creators during the assessment.
