# Product research — 27 October 2026

Sources: https://incubrix.com/ and https://app.incubrix.com/. The public app entry point was reachable but exposed only a client-rendered shell to this environment. An authenticated product session and the candidate's invitation were unavailable. No upload, edit, export, integration or scheduling action is claimed tested.

Five observations from the public positioning:

1. The workflow covers source content through clips, captions, narration and posts. Test one complete source-to-export journey in the authenticated product.
2. Topic discovery is presented as niche-aware. Test whether recommendations include useful source evidence and match a selected creator segment.
3. Editing is described through transcript deletion. Test timing, speaker boundaries and export fidelity before using that promise in a demonstration.
4. Publishing names five destinations. Test connection scopes, scheduling timezones and partial-failure recovery with owned accounts.
5. Feature availability messaging is inconsistent: navigation describes performance as rolling out, while its main section says coming soon. Confirm availability and use one consistent status in outreach.

Three acquisition hypotheses:

- A reviewed episode-specific transformation preview may be more persuasive than generic feature copy. Compare positive replies after a permitted pilot; no result is assumed.
- Creator-led podcasts offer an observable recurring input. Start with creators who also have evidence of a relevant content workflow need and invited business contact.
- Evidence expiry, opt-out handling and accurate availability claims can reduce wasted outreach. Track contact errors and suppression timing alongside replies.

These are research notes and proposed tests. Replace them with five evidenced product observations after the authenticated walkthrough. Public marketing claims do not establish that the product features have been verified to work.
